QUESTIONS

The straight answers.

How do I know which tier I need?
Most clients start with Greenhouse — single location, $500K to $3M revenue, 30 to 150 leads per month. Orchard fits multi-trade businesses or single locations doing $3M to $10M with higher lead volume. Estate is built for $10M+ businesses running three or more locations. The audit call sorts this out. We're not trying to upsell you into the wrong tier.
How does billing actually work?
For Greenhouse: $2,500 at signing covers the build and your first 30 days. $1,997/month begins on Day 31, all-inclusive. For Orchard: $5,000 setup, $4,997/month, same structure. Estate is custom — we have a discovery conversation first. All three are month-to-month after the first 60 days. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. Setup fees are one-time.
What does the guarantee actually mean?
We install your system in 7 days. For the next 60 days we track every extra job the system books that you wouldn't have booked without it. If the count is under 5, we keep working — at no additional cost — until you get there. No refund games. No fine print. We work until it works.
What if I already use a CRM like ServiceTitan or Jobber?
Good. We integrate with what you have. ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, GoHighLevel, JobNimbus, AccuLynx, FieldPulse, and more. No rip-and-replace. The GRAIN layer sits on top of your existing tools and makes them do what you hoped they would when you bought them.
Will the voice answering actually work for my customers?
It's not trying to trick anyone. Our agents introduce themselves as your booking assistant. They handle routine questions like hours, services, basic pricing, and scheduling. Anything complex gets handed to a human. Most homeowners would rather talk to a helpful agent at 9pm than leave a voicemail that nobody returns until Tuesday.
What does the audit involve?
A 30-minute call. We ask about your lead sources, average job value, current follow-up process, and the tools you use. You leave with a specific dollar figure of what your business is losing per year. We use the call to determine if your business is a fit for GRAIN. If it isn't, the analysis is yours either way.
Why are you selective about who you work with?
Because the standards we hold are demanding. Every call answered in 60 seconds. Every quote followed up for 90 days. Every system live in 7 days. To maintain that, we work with a small number of home service businesses at a time. The audit determines fit on both sides.
What's the typical ROI?
Most clients book their first GRAIN-attributed extra job within the first 14 days. Average client adds 5 to 12 extra jobs per month from previously lost leads. For a roofer at a $12,500 average ticket, that's $62K to $150K in monthly recovered revenue. Against a $1,997 monthly fee.
What if I don't have many leads to work with?
GRAIN works best for businesses generating at least 30 leads per month from any combination of marketing, referrals, and inbound calls. If you're below that, you may need lead generation support before GRAIN is the right fit. The audit will tell us either way.
