QUESTIONS

The straight answers.

How do I know which tier I need?
Most clients start with Greenhouse — single location, $500K to $3M revenue, 30 to 150 leads per month. Orchard fits multi-trade businesses or single locations doing $3M to $10M with higher lead volume. Estate is built for $10M+ businesses running three or more locations. The audit call sorts this out. We're not trying to upsell you into the wrong tier.
How does billing actually work?
For Greenhouse: $3,500 at signing covers the build and your first 30 days. $2,497/month begins on Day 31, all-inclusive. For Orchard: $7,500 setup, $5,997/month, same structure. Estate is custom — we have a discovery conversation first. All three are month-to-month after the first 60 days. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. Setup fees are one-time. We default new clients to ACH for the monthly to save you the credit card fees, but card works fine if you prefer.
What does the guarantee actually mean?
We install your system in 7 days. For the next 60 days we track every extra job the system books that you wouldn't have booked without it. If the count is under 5, we keep working — at no additional cost — until you get there. No refund games. No fine print. The full methodology is published — we'll show you exactly how we count a job as ours.
What's the Greenhouse dashboard?
A live performance dashboard at your own subdomain — yourname.grainmethod.com. Built on your own data. Live lead pipeline, every call answered with the recording, every quote followed up with the status, every dollar attributed to the system. Plus Ask GRAIN — a chat interface where you can ask questions about your own business in plain English and get real answers. No logging into someone else's CRM. Your business, your dashboard.
Do I need to switch CRMs?
No. GRAIN plugs into whatever you're already using — ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, JobNimbus, AccuLynx, FieldPulse, GoHighLevel, Leap, and others. Nothing rips out. Your existing system stays. We become the operating layer on top.
What if my business doesn't fit any of these tiers?
If you're under $500K revenue, the math probably doesn't work yet. If you're between tiers, the audit call will help you choose. If you're a franchise or have unusual structure, that's an Estate conversation — email us directly with subject "Estate Tier Inquiry."
Can I add Add-Ons later?
Yes. Add or drop any add-on month to month. The base tier and add-ons are billed separately. We'll never push an add-on you don't need — they're listed publicly so you can pick what fits.
