QUESTIONS

The honest answers.

How is this different from hiring a marketing agency?
A marketing agency gets you more leads. That's the top of the funnel. GRAIN handles what happens after the lead comes in. The follow-up, the response time, the nurture. Most of our clients keep their marketing agency and add us underneath it.
What if I already use a CRM like ServiceTitan or Jobber?
Good. We integrate with what you have. ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, GoHighLevel, JobNimbus, AccuLynx, FieldPulse, and more. No rip-and-replace. The GRAIN layer sits on top and makes the tools do what you hoped they would when you bought them.
How does the 5-job guarantee actually work?
We install the system in 7 days. For the next 60 days we track every extra job the system books that you wouldn't have booked without it. If the count is under 5, we keep working free until you get them. No refunds — we keep working. That's the commitment.
Is this AI answering my calls, or real people?
The voice agent is a system, but it's trained on thousands of real home service conversations in your specific trade. It introduces itself as your booking assistant. It can answer routine questions, book appointments, escalate emergencies, and qualify leads. Anything complex gets handed to a human. Most homeowners would rather talk to a helpful agent at 9pm than leave a voicemail nobody returns until Tuesday.
What does the free audit involve?
A 30-minute call. We ask about your lead sources, average job value, current follow-up process, and the tools you use. You leave with a specific dollar figure of what your business is losing per year. Whether or not you hire us.
I'm in Nashville. Do you work locally?
Yes. Nashville is home base. We work with home service businesses across Middle Tennessee including Franklin, Brentwood, Mt. Juliet, Murfreesboro, and Hendersonville. We serve clients nationally too. The system works the same whether you're in Nashville or Nebraska.
