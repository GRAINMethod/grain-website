Common questions

The fine print, spelled out.

How do I know which tier I need?

One location? You're a Greenhouse. Two to nine locations, or multiple trades? You're an Orchard. Ten or more locations, or a platform rolling up shops? You're an Estate. Not sure? Apply for the audit. We'll tell you in 30 minutes, honestly, even if it means a smaller deal for us.

How does billing actually work?

Nothing is charged at signing. At go-live we charge your setup and first month, then auto-charge monthly by card or ACH. The 10% revival share is billed monthly, only on revived jobs completed and paid. Annual prepay saves you two months: pay 10 months up front for 12 months of service.

What if it doesn't pay for itself?

That's the Payback Guarantee. If revived jobs haven't covered everything you've paid by day 90, your monthly pauses until they do. If it's still short at day 180, we refund the difference. It applies to shops with $1.5M+ in unsold estimates from the last 24 months, with revived appointments run within 2 business days and jobs marked completed and paid.

Can I cancel anytime?

After the first 90 days, yes. 30 days' written notice. No penalties, no clawback. If GRAIN stops working for you, you should leave.

What if I'm on the annual prepay and need to cancel partway through?

Same policy. 30 days' written notice. We refund the unused months at the standard monthly rate: you keep the months you used at the regular price, and the rest comes back to you. No clawback on setup. The annual prepay is a price discount, not a lock-in.

Can I move between tiers?

Up or down, with 30 days' notice. Each location you add goes live on its own 14-day build.

What's included in setup vs monthly?

Setup covers system design, build, configuration and integration. Monthly covers ongoing operation, tuning, upgrades and support. Setup is one-time. Monthly is recurring until you cancel.
