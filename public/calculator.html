How we calculate this

Slow response loss = 32% of monthly leads × close rate × avg job value × value of recoverable response window (HBR Lead Response Study, ServiceTitan benchmarks). Follow-up loss = the difference between the 1.3-touch industry average close rate (~18%) and the 5-7-touch benchmark (~32%). Past-customer value = estimated customer list (revenue × 5 years ÷ avg job value) × 8% reactivation rate, monthly amortized. These are conservative directional figures.
